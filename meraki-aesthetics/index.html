Good to know

Your questions, answered

I’m new to aesthetics. Where should I start?

Book a free consultation. Nurse Charlie will listen to what you would like to achieve, explain suitable options and answer your questions without pressure to go ahead.

Is the clinic private?

Yes. Meraki is a private home clinic and appointments are one-to-one and confidential. There is no waiting room, so please arrive at your appointment time rather than early.

Can I bring my child?

Well-behaved children may attend if childcare is difficult. Please remember this is a clinical setting where sharps are present, so they must remain safely supervised.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations with less than 24 hours’ notice require 50% of the treatment cost. Please give at least 48 hours’ notice to reschedule through your online booking portal.

Will my result look natural?

Natural and subtle results are central to Charlie’s approach. Your treatment plan is based on your own features, clinical suitability and goals; individual results and recovery can vary.

Appointment terms

By booking, you agree to Meraki Aesthetics’ cancellation and rescheduling policy. Treatment is subject to consultation, medical history and clinical suitability. Please use the customer portal to manage an existing booking or contact the clinic if your circumstances change.
