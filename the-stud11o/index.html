The Studio

Roll out your mat, and let the pace drop.

The Studio is the quiet side of the building. Our classes build core strength, mobility, flexibility and control, so you move better, recover well and feel steadier in everything else you do.

Every class adapts to you, whether it's your first time on the mat or your fiftieth. No pressure to keep up, just space to move with intention.

New to HYROX? Here's the whole race.
Run 1kmOne stationRepeat ×8

Eight one-kilometre runs, each split by a functional workout: sled push, sled pull, burpee broad jumps, rowing, farmer's carry, sandbag lunges, wall balls. Same race worldwide, your time on the global board.

New to it? HYROX Lite runs Thursday evenings, built for exactly this, and every workout scales to where you are today.
